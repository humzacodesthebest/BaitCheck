    If you already clicked

    1. Stop interacting. Do not send codes, passwords, or money.
    2. Contact the organization through its official app, saved number, or typed website.
    3. If you entered a password, change it from a trusted device and enable MFA.
    4. Contact your bank immediately if payment details or money were involved. Report the message through your carrier or local cybercrime authority.

    How to interpret results

    High-confidence signal means the address or request has a strong structural warning, such as a deceptive domain.

    Context signal means a tactic like urgency or a prize claim. It is useful evidence but can appear in legitimate messages too.

    No signal found never means safe. Verify independently before signing in, paying, or sharing a code.

    Built for education and safer decisions. BaitCheck uses local heuristics only; it has no live reputation data and cannot detect every scam.
